How to Play

Deck: 20 cards (A,K,Q,J,10 in 4 suits) + 1 Joker (wild).

Roles: Each round one player is Starter (S), the other Follower (F). Roles swap every round.

Forced bets: S posts 2b, F posts 1b.

Pre-draw: F acts first — Call, Raise, or Fold.

Draw: F discards 0–5 cards and draws replacements; S sees the count, then does the same.

Post-draw: S acts first — Check, Raise, Call, or Fold.

Showdown: Best 5-card hand wins. Joker becomes whichever card maximizes the holder's hand.

Hand ranks (high → low): Five of a Kind → Royal Flush → Four of a Kind → Full House → Straight → Three of a Kind → Two Pair → Pair.
